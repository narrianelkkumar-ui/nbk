FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Which AutoCAD versions are supported? ▼
Brahmanee supports all versions of AutoCAD from 2010 through 2026, as well as Civil 3D and AutoCAD Map 3D on Windows.
Does Brahmanee require an active internet connection? ▼
No. Brahmanee uses hardware-based offline device lock (MAC Address & Hard Drive ID security). Once activated, it works 100% offline in field environments.
How are land areas calculated for Telangana revenue maps? ▼
Brahmanee automatically converts CAD polyline areas into Acres, Guntas, and Gajalu (Square Yards) following Telangana land revenue calculation standards.
